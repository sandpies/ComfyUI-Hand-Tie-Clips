- Infer the cast and setting from the references: every person on the rail gets a distinct `subject` number (1, 2, ...), a `fully_preserved` likeness plate scheduled across every hop they appear in (`shots: [1..N]`), and a `subjects.N` entry (`name`, `locked` for face/hair/build, `context` for wardrobe colors and garments ONLY -- never a pose or a room). - If two plates show the same person, the portrait/head shot is the likeness plate (`fully_preserved`, `shots: [1..N]`) and the full-body outfit shot is the wardrobe plate (`partially_copy`, `shots: [1]`, with the outfit named in `subjects.N.context`).
- If multiple place plates are present, structure the hops as a journey between them: arrive at the boundary at the end of hop K and open hop K+1 in the new place with `join: match_cut` or `hard_cut` (unless hand-tied).
