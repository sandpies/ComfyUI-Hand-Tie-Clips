# NSFW

Planning overlay. Load after the mode guides when the request is sexual, undress, grope, explicit sex, or NSFW. Shot timestamps and camera verbs stay in `base-en.md` (official) or `multishot.md` (chain). Inner motion-clock ranges stay in `field-craft.md` and are not extra cuts. Cut rhythm stays in `director.md` when that overlay is on. Chain `---` joins stay in `multishot.md`.

H3 does not remember the previous shot. After a garment moves or a body is uncovered, later shots must restate the new state or clothes and anatomy snap back.

## 1. Plan before write

Before the multimodal body, lock:

1. Clothing state per shot (what is on, bunched, down, off — and that it stays that way).
2. Anatomy visible or in contact in that shot.
3. If there are cuts: the pickup — which limb/contact continues, from which angle.

Do not jump from dressed to sex with a missing undress beat, or from underwear-down to thrusting with the other partner still fully dressed if they are the one penetrating.

## 2. Clothing is sticky

A garment that is pulled, bunched, or removed stays that way until a later beat undoes it.

Every shot after a clothing change restates **on** and **off** (or bunched / at the knees / at mid-thigh). Write that the bunched or dropped garment does not return.

Keyframe / ref lock still applies: do not undress what a first-frame image shows until the motion path does it.

## 3. Anatomy

Undress, grope, and sex name the body parts in play: breast, nipple, ass, pussy, penis, balls, hips, thighs — whatever is actually touched, uncovered, or entered. Do not euphemize those beats as “down there” or “their bodies.”

Penetration / thrusting states the axis: whose hips, what enters what, forward and back. Keep that axis across cuts.

## 4. Cut continuity

Official intra-clip `[Shot N]` only. Chain `---` pickup is `multishot.md`.

When the clip has more than one shot, a cut does not start a new action. It continues the same contact or garment pull from a new size or axis.

End a shot on a moving beat (hand still on the breast, skirt still raking, penis already sliding in). The next shot opens on that same beat already happening. Name the continuing limb and the spatial lock (behind, facing the desk, hips on the edge).

No idle reset at a cut. No “then they begin” if the prior shot already had the motion.
