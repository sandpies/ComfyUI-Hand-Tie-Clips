# Coverage menu

Planner menu for the storyboard. Load with `planning.md` when shots are not yet chosen. At Write, open it again only to copy the Paste cell of a row the confirmed board actually chose.

Official verb names stay in `base-en.md`. Paste the cell in the camera sentence. Do not paste a film title, a rig name, or a sentence from the Wrong column.

Prefer a plain official verb: push, pull, pan, truck, tilt, pedestal, arc, tracking, shake, POV, roll, with amplitude and speed. Use a row only when the beat needs that result. One primary move per shot (`failures.md`). Dolly zoom is the one opposed pair, written as one path.

When `official-looks.md` is on, its beat recipe wins. Do not replace a look's specified move with a row.

A `---` chain may use glide, locked face, crane, match on action, occupied edges, and practical light. It may not use dolly zoom, crash zoom, or whip (`multishot.md` forbids fast complex motion). When `anime-2d.md` is on, skip every row marked optical. A 2D sting is an impact frame and speed lines from that file.

## Dolly zoom

Once in a clip, when the person must stay the same size while the place becomes untrustworthy. Optical. Not on a chain.

Wrong result: the person grows (a push) or only the crop tightens (a zoom).

Paste: The camera pulls out while the lens zooms in, so [subject]'s head stays the same size and the [background] compresses.

Reverse paste: The camera pushes in while the lens zooms out, so [subject]'s head stays the same size and the [background] stretches.

## Crash zoom

Punctuate one fact — a face, a weapon, a joke — once in that beat. Optical. Not on a chain.

Wrong result: the camera walks closer and the background slides sideways.

Paste: The camera zooms in with large amplitude at fast speed, body in place, radial blur through the snap, and lands tight on [detail] while [breath, cloth, or a blink] continues.

Do not write HOLD, static, or freeze. The tight frame stays alive by the continuing motion named in the paste.

## Whip pan

Snap attention to a new aim. Not on a chain.

Wrong result: a readable fast pan, or radial zoom blur.

Paste: The camera pans [left or right] with large amplitude at fast speed, the middle of the pan an unreadable horizontal streak, then a sharp stop on [landing].

Anime: the drawing streaks sideways. No shutter, lens, or blur wording.

## Crane up / crane down

Leave or arrive at a person so new geography appears. A tilt leaves the camera in place and shows no new ground. Write it as one path.

Paste, up: The camera rises and eases back from [start height]. [Subject] stays in frame and becomes smaller as [roofs, street, or crowd] come into view.

Paste, down: The camera descends from [wide] toward [face or object]. The same person stays visible the whole way.

Do not name a crane, jib, or Technocrane.

## Rack focus

Hand attention from one distance to another inside a still frame. Optical. The head sits on purpose. Director's moving-camera rule yields for this shot only.

Wrong result: a cut, a morph, or both planes staying sharp.

Paste: The camera stays at the same height and distance. Focus leaves the sharp [near object] and arrives on [far subject] over about two seconds. Both keep their shape.

Do not add a zoom or a push in that sentence.

Do not write HOLD, static, or freeze.

## Glide

An interior state carries the body. The feet do not take steps.

Wrong result: a normal gait, or a push that enlarges a planted person.

Paste: [Subject] stays upright, both feet planted, the same size in frame, while the [corridor] streams backward.

Do not name a platform or a dolly. Anime: the painted background scrolls. The figure is a held cel.

## Locked face

Panic or dissociation. The audience sees the person, not the street they see.

Wrong result: a POV of the street, a follow from behind, or a camera prop.

Paste: [Subject]'s face and shoulders stay centered and the same size while the [background] bobs and shears with each step.

Do not write body-mounted, Snorri, or camera.

## Match on action

Change size or angle on one gesture. `director.md` already starts the incoming shot mid-motion. This row is the outgoing half.

Paste: [Hand] starts [the reach or swing] and is still traveling at the cut. The next shot continues that same hand in the same screen direction and finishes the contact.

## Frame and light

Storyboard notes, not moves. Skip on a product hold or a formal single.

Occupied edges: name the occluder and the edge, and leave a hole for the face. A soft oval that is not an object is a vignette.

Practical: name the fixture in frame — lamp, neon, candle, or screen — and which side of the face it hits. A dark fixture with the face lit from the lens is a miss. Dominant-source wording for Astro stays in `astro-cinema.md`.

## Storyboard line

One line per shot: time range, size, plain verb or row name, and the new information the shot adds. Add an edge or light note only when the shot needs it. Micromovement clocks are not shots.

## Self-check

Fix the board or the paste if:

- a row is a second equal move in the same shot (dolly zoom is the opposed pair, one path)
- dolly zoom, crash zoom, or whip is on a chain
- an optical row is on under anime-2d
- the paste names a rig, a film, or a negative
- a crane shows no new geography
- HOLD, static, or freeze appears in a crash-zoom or rack paste
- a row replaces a move that `official-looks.md` already specifies
